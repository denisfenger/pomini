Common questions

My purchase did not unlock Pro. Open Pomini's settings, tap the Pomini Pro row and use Restore purchases. If it still does not unlock, email us from the address you can be reached at and we will sort it out.

I want my data removed. Pomini keeps your data only on your device. Deleting the app removes it; the reset option in statistics removes your history while keeping the app.

Sounds do not play when the app is closed. iOS plays Pomini's sounds through notifications. Check that notifications are allowed for Pomini in iOS Settings, and that Sound is on inside Pomini's settings.
