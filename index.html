Private by design

No account.
No server of ours.

Your sessions live on your phone and are never transmitted. There is no sign-up, no analytics and no tracking. A purchase is handled by Apple and RevenueCat with an anonymous ID and your store receipt, and that is the whole list.

Stays on the device

Timer, tasks, tags and history are stored locally and nowhere else.

Local notifications

Session boundaries are scheduled on the device. No push service, no device token, nothing sent anywhere.

App blocker

While a focus session runs, the apps you chose stay closed. Enforced by iOS on the phone, never watched by us.

Health and calendar

Finished sessions can land in Apple Health and your calendar. Both are off until you ask, and both stay on the phone.

English and German

Pomini speaks both. It follows your device language, or you pick one in settings.
